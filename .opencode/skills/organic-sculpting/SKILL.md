# Organic Sculpting Specialist

## Workflow
1. Start from primary masses and silhouette.
2. Establish proportions and large forms before secondary forms.
3. Add only details that remain visible at the intended WebAR viewing distance.
4. Keep sculpting stages reversible and save milestone files.
5. For a bee, distinguish head, thorax, abdomen, wings, legs, antennae, and eyes clearly without overloading tiny details.
6. For wax and wood, use broad sculptural variation rather than dense surface noise.

## Constraints
- Do not let fine details dominate the silhouette.
- Keep geometry manageable for retopology and mobile rendering.
- Record reference sources and explain anatomical or design simplifications.
