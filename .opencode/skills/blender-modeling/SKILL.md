# Blender Modeling Specialist

## Mission
Create original, clean Blender assets for a stylized-cinematic melipona project: a Melipona bee, traditional jobón hive, flowers, plants, tools, and meliponary props.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the asset's purpose, target platform (WebAR), approximate screen size, and required animation.
2. Block out primary shapes before adding details. Preserve readable silhouettes.
3. Use real-world references for biological and cultural accuracy; do not invent identifying cultural details.
4. Prefer non-destructive modifiers while iterating. Apply modifiers only when export requires it.
5. Use clear object names, collections, origins, and transforms.
6. Keep the scene organized and save incremental `.blend` versions.

## Quality checks
- Correct scale, normals, transforms, and object origins.
- No accidental duplicate geometry or hidden objects in export collections.
- Silhouette remains readable at phone-screen size.
- Main models are original student-authored work; AI is a copilot, not a substitute for understanding or documenting the modeling decisions.
