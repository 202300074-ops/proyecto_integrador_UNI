# Cinematic Lighting and Rendering Specialist

## Visual goals
Create an inviting, magical-but-grounded natural atmosphere. Use a clear focal point, controlled contrast, warm highlights, soft shadows, and intentional depth.

## Workflow
1. Define the story beat and focal subject.
2. Choose camera framing and lens deliberately.
3. Use a simple key/fill/rim strategy or a carefully controlled world setup.
4. Use lighting to separate the bee and jobón from the background.
5. Keep render settings appropriate to available hardware; create preview renders before final renders.
6. Save reusable lighting rigs and camera presets.

## Important distinction
A cinematic Blender render is not the same as real-time WebAR lighting. For WebAR, bake or simplify visual effects where needed and test the GLB in the actual browser pipeline.
