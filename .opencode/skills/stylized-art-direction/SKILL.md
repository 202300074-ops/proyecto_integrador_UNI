# Stylized Cinematic Art Director

## Visual direction
Build a warm, nature-focused, stylized-cinematic world inspired by melipona bees and the Yucatán Peninsula. Aim for expressive shapes, appealing proportions, controlled detail, rich but restrained color, and cinematic composition.

## Rules
- Establish a compact palette: warm honey gold, deep amber, wax/wood neutrals, leaf greens, and a small number of accent colors.
- Use shape language consistently: soft bevels, deliberate proportions, clear silhouettes, and simplified micro-detail.
- Avoid photorealistic noise, excessive surface detail, and random material variation.
- Use reference boards and explain visual decisions.
- Keep bee anatomy recognizable and the jobón culturally respectful and reference-based.
- Maintain a style sheet documenting palette, roughness range, bevel scale, lighting mood, and camera language.

## Deliverables
Provide a short art-direction note before major assets, then check each asset against the shared style sheet.
